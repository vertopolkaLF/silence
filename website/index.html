Global hotkeys

Every action gets its own key.

Keyboard, mouse side buttons, or a controller. Your mute key works from any window.

  • Toggle, force mute, force unmute, and hold actions
  • As many shortcuts per action as you want
  • Switch audio devices and open Settings by hotkey
Click anywhere to mute and unmute

Visual overlay

Always know if you're muted.

A small always-on-top badge shows your mic state over every app. Style it, move it, or click it.

  • Mic icon, colored dot, or icon with text
  • Show always, only when muted, or right after a toggle
  • Doubles as an on-screen mic button
  • Size, opacity, colors, and icon packs are all yours

Sound on, then click the mic

Sounds

Hear the moment you mute.

8 built-in sound themes, or your own MP3, WAV, OGG, and FLAC files.

  • Different sounds for mute and unmute
  • Separate volume for every action
  • Hold actions can get their own sounds

Got a controller? Plug it in and press LB + A

Muted

Gamepad support

Your controller is a mute button now.

Bind any button or combo. The keyboard can stay on the other side of the room.

  • Button combos like LB + A
  • Xbox and PlayStation controllers, even racing wheels
  • Every hotkey action works on the controller too

More tricks.

Hold to talk

Hold a key to unmute, let go to mute. Or flip it and hold to cough.

Auto-mute

Mutes when the app starts or after you go AFK. Unmutes when you are back, if you want.

Every mic at once

Mute one exact microphone, or all of them with a single press.

Device switching

Swap the default input or output with a hotkey or from the tray menu.

Panic key

Force mute only ever mutes. Smash it twice, you are still muted.

Tray status

The logo, a mic, or a colored dot next to the clock shows your state.
